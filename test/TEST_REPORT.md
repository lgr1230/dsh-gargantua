## Known limitations (documented, not failures)

* Headless rAF is vsync-capped at 60 Hz, so `[GARGANTUA_BENCH]` numbers from
  headless runs are not representative of real GPU frame times.
* The SwiftShader fallback path renders correctly but is slow (software
  rasterization); real devices get the GPU path. The app auto-detects
  SwiftShader and switches to the Standard preset.
* Schwarzschild spacetime only (no Kerr spin) — as specified.
